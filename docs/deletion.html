Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 2026

What Data We Hold

AlongXide may hold the following data associated with your account or usage:

  • Name, phone number, and delivery address
  • Order history and transaction records
  • Device information used during app sessions

We do not store any Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok user personal data. Our Meta integration is used exclusively to publish content to our own official social media accounts.

How to Request Deletion

Option 1 — Email request (fastest):

  1. Send an email to alongxide@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request
  3. Include your name and the phone number associated with your account
  4. We will confirm deletion within 7 business days

What Gets Deleted

Upon a confirmed deletion request, we will permanently remove:

  • Your account profile and contact details
  • Your order history and delivery addresses
  • Any preferences or settings linked to your account

Anonymised transaction records may be retained for legal and accounting purposes for up to 12 months, as required by Nigerian financial regulations.

Facebook / Meta Data

AlongXide uses the Facebook Ad Library API solely to access publicly available, anonymised ad data for competitive market research. We do not store any Facebook user personal data, and no Facebook user data deletion requests are applicable to our integration.

Contact

For any data-related questions, email us at alongxide@gmail.com.
