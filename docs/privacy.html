Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use AlongXide, we may collect:

  • Name, phone number, and delivery address
  • Order history and transaction details
  • Device information and approximate location (for delivery matching)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Communicate order status and updates
  • Improve our service quality and runner matching
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform safety

3. TikTok Integration

AlongXide uses TikTok's Content Posting API solely to publish promotional content to our official TikTok account (@alongxide). We do not collect, store, or process any TikTok user data beyond our own account credentials. No third-party TikTok user data is accessed or stored.

4. Facebook / Meta Integration

AlongXide uses the Facebook and Instagram Graph API to publish promotional content to our official Facebook Page and Instagram account. We also use the Facebook Ad Library API (ads_read) solely to access publicly available, anonymised ad data for competitive market research purposes — specifically to understand messaging trends in the Nigerian delivery and errand service industry.

We do not collect, store, or process any Facebook or Instagram user personal data. No advertiser account data, private user information, or audience data is accessed or stored. All Ad Library data is used transiently for analysis and immediately discarded — only the generated insights are retained internally.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with runners assigned to your order and with payment processors as necessary to complete transactions.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information. All sensitive credentials are stored securely and never shared publicly.

6. Data Retention

We retain order data for up to 12 months for record-keeping purposes. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at alongxide@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our service evolves. We will notify users of significant changes via our social media channels.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact alongxide@gmail.com.
